Research

2026 – present

Graduate Researcher

Dr. Lucy Owen's lab | University of Montana

  • Built the analysis pipeline for a first-author paper on multi-subject archetypal analysis (MS-AA) of naturalistic fMRI from 87 participants: fits at every model order in two orientations (up to 700 components, 10 seeds each), participant-held-out decoding, PCA benchmarks and simulations, run at scale on the University of Montana's Hellgate HPC cluster with Slurm and Apptainer.
  • Released that pipeline as a reproducible, tested package (msaa-paper) with pinned environments and Make targets that rebuild every figure and source table from the archived data.
  • Ported SuperEEG, my advisor's doctoral project, to GPyTorch and modern Python 3.12 / NumPy 2 (supereeg-gpyt), so its Gaussian process models build on the GPU, and wrote a converter for files in the old format.
  • Thesis project: applying archetypal analysis and timecorr to fMRI from trauma-exposed individuals, to look for shared signatures of exposure, resilience and recovery.
